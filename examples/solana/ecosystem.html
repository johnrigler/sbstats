SB Stats / Solana ecosystem map

A technical guide to the interfaces surrounding a Solana token. These are different roles, not exclusive company categories. All sites are third parties; links are not endorsements.

Live demonstration: one mint, multiple views

  1. Select an active token in SB Stats candidate explorer. A boosted listing is paid discovery, not evidence of merit.
  2. Copy its exact mint address. Symbols and names are ambiguous.
  3. Inspect the mint, token accounts, authorities, ownership concentration, and relevant on-chain programs in an explorer.
  4. Open its market listing and identify the pair/pool address, DEX, liquidity, volume window, and available route. Distinguish reported figures from independently verified chain data.
  5. Observe the token's claimed website, X, and Telegram links. A link is an unverified association, not evidence of organizer identity or coordination.
  6. Connect a wallet only to a verified domain. Differentiate wallet connection, message-signing login, transaction authorization, and program execution.
  7. For a trade demonstration, start with a read-only quote and transaction simulation; optionally sign a small normal swap. Match the transaction signature on SB Stats, the explorer, and the public feed. Do not manufacture activity or manipulate prices.
  8. Transition to Mogwai: the public ledger remains accessible independently of any single trading interface. Cross-chain representations and reconstructed indexes require explicit protocol definitions and verification.

Separate engineering tracks

Observation

SB Stats

Read-only Solana discovery, exact-mint indexing, pool observations, wallet and trade research, and reproducible analytics. Future AI research can propose hypotheses; deterministic code should perform verification and enforce limits.

Execution research

Agent / swaps / liquidity

Quote, simulate, decode instructions, calculate network/program/platform/LP costs, optionally submit wallet-authorized swaps. Position management and LP simulation belong in separate modules, not the discovery collector.

Issuance

Token creation is different

On Solana, an SPL or Token-2022 mint and its authorities, metadata, accounts and liquidity configuration must be designed. Minting a token does not automatically create a market, liquidity, or demand.
